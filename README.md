Market Brew provides AI-driven visibility modeling for brands, helping them understand how search and large language models evaluate their presence. The platform captures structural and behavioral signals, aligns them with real customer intent, and offers predictive insights to improve SEO, content strategy, and AI visibility. Trusted by top brands, Market Brew enables enterprises to model and optimize their digital architecture for better search rankings and AI-driven discovery.

**Areas:** AI · SEO · Marketing · Visibility · LLM

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages. From that it derives the properties above — contracts split per API, workflows, schemas, collections, governance rules, plans and rate limits — and scores them against the published [Kin Score rubric](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score).

The artifacts are published on [APIs.io](https://apis.io), not in this repository. This repository is the public record for Market Brew: the place to say something is wrong, missing or out of date, and to ask for a re-score or a removal.
